A local partner for your rental

Your home, looked after.

What we handle

Owner services

The practical work of keeping a rental occupied, maintained, and accounted for.

Tenant placement

We market the home, show it, and present a qualified applicant so the property is not sitting empty longer than it needs to.

Screening

Applicants are screened before move-in. You see the recommendation and stay part of the decision on who lives in your property.

Rent collection

Rent is collected, recorded, and deposited through trust accounting that follows Washington and Idaho rules. You can see what came in.

Maintenance

When something breaks, we take the call, coordinate the repair with local vendors, and tell you what was done.

Inspections

We inspect the property so small issues are caught before they become expensive ones, and so you know the condition of the home.

Accounting

Owner statements show income, expenses, and the balance. Trust accounting stays separate from our operating funds, as state rules require.

Why Porch & Pine

A neighbor, not a call center

We live and work in this region. When you call, you reach the people who manage the property, not a national queue.

  • Licensed property management in Washington and Idaho
  • Trust accounting and financial reporting that follow state rules
  • Fair housing practices on every application and tenancy
  • A local vendor network for repairs
  • Clear statements, so the money is never a surprise

If you want a manager who will say what is happening at the house, that is the relationship we are built for.
