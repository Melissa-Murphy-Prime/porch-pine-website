About Porch & Pine

Local property management rooted in the Pacific Northwest

Our Story

Porch & Pine Properties was founded with a simple belief: property management should feel personal, not transactional. We work with owners who want a trusted partner, not just a vendor — and tenants who want to feel genuinely at home.

We operate across Washington and Idaho, managing everything from long-term residential rentals to short-term vacation properties. Every property we manage gets the same level of attention and care.

As a licensed property management company, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of trust accounting and financial transparency required by state regulations — so owners always know exactly where their money is.
